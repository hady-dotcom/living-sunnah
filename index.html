Qareen — a dark violet haze at the shoulder. Bigger and darker = stronger pull. It can only delay, diminish or divert; the person always chooses. It shrinks at the adhan, in congregation, after the morning remembrance, after Ayat al-Kursi, and to a third in Ramadan.

Sunnah World

A make-believe town shaped like Mississauga, where about 750 people live by the Sunnah clock. The sky drives everything: real sunrise, sunset and prayer times for Mississauga, the real moon phase, and the Islamic (Hijri) date, which starts at sunset.

Each person is different: their practice level (from Surah Fatir 35:32), their habits, health, honesty, phone habits, mood, disabilities, job and family. They wake, pray, fast, work, visit neighbours, argue, make up and sleep. Some do really well; some struggle.

Three eras: switch between Mississauga in 1986, 2006 and 2026 at the top. Each era repeats its year forever, on the same date as today. All three keep living, even when this page is closed — when you come back it catches up. Every night each era prints its own newspaper, people talk about it (letters in 1986, forums and MSN in 2006, social media in 2026), and once a month a pretend survey checks how everyone is feeling. 2026 is a forecast. All numbers are simulated.

Drag to move · scroll or pinch to zoom · click a person to open their day · space to pause.

This is a simulation, not a fatwa. It never judges anyone's soul or works out reward. The Qareen and angels are shown as soft lights with no faces, only to picture the rules in the source sheets. Places are inspired by real Mississauga; positions are approximate. Times use the ISNA 15° method, and Hijri dates use the Umm al-Qura calculation (with local-sighting masjids sometimes one day behind).

Where the daily activities come from: what people do hour by hour in each era is based on broad patterns in Statistics Canada's General Social Survey on Time Use (1986, 1992, 1998, 2005, 2010 and 2015 cycles, and the 2022 survey) and the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics American Time Use Survey (2003 onward): TV is the biggest leisure activity in every era; in 1986 people also used landlines, radio, newspapers, VCRs and visited in person; home computers, MSN and flip phones arrive by 2006; phones, streaming and delivery apps dominate by 2026. Exact minutes are not copied — the patterns are simplified, so treat them as estimates.

Both angels are good. The right angel writes good deeds; the left angel writes bad deeds but waits, giving time to turn back. Only the Qareen tempts. Incidents (🎬) are made-up situations from a library of 100+ templates.
